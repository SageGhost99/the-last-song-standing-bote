How to Play

  • What it is: A tournament-style bracket where Taylor Swift songs face off to find your ultimate favorite!
  • Song count: 64 songs are randomly selected from all 229 songs across 12 albums.
  • How to choose: Click your preferred song in each matchup. Winners automatically advance to the next round.
  • How long: Takes about 10-15 minutes to complete all 63 matchups and crown your champion!
  • Play again: Click "Start New Bracket" to shuffle and discover a new winner with different songs!
